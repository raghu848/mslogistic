FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

GOT QUESTIONS?

Clear answers to common logistics and shipping inquiries.

What logistics services does MS LOGISTIC provide?
MS LOGISTIC provides comprehensive international freight forwarding, including air freight, ocean container shipping (FCL/LCL), road transportation, climate-controlled warehousing, customs brokerage, express courier, and specialized project heavy logistics.
How can I request a quotation for my shipment?
You can fill out our online quotation form on this website or click "GET A QUOTE". Provide origin, destination, cargo weight/dimensions, and required service, and our team will issue a competitive quote within 2 hours.
What information is required for an international shipment?
Standard requirements include Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin, Shipper & Consignee contact details, and any product-specific compliance certificates for customs clearance.
Do you handle international ocean and air freight?
Yes, MS LOGISTIC operates across 50+ countries worldwide with direct vessel allocations and commercial airline freight space across Asia, Europe, North America, Middle East, and Africa.
Can I track my shipment in real time?
Yes! Enter your Waybill or Tracking Code in the search bar on our website hero or tracking section to view live milestone updates from origin pickup to final destination delivery.
Do you provide customs clearance services?
Our licensed customs brokers manage complete import and export documentation, duty calculations, tariff classification, and regulatory compliance to ensure fast clearance without border delays.
Do you provide secure warehousing and storage?
We offer short-term and long-term secure warehousing, bonded storage, inventory management, palletizing, order fulfillment, and temperature-controlled storage options.
How are shipping costs calculated?
Freight costs depend on chargeable weight (actual vs dimensional weight), transport mode (air, sea, road), lane distance, fuel surcharges, and any additional services like customs handling or insurance.
